Who we are

sbtwork ("we", "us") publishes mobile games for Android on Google Play. This one policy applies to every game listed below. In this policy, "the Game" means whichever of our games you are playing.

By installing or playing the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, or store personal information about the people who play our games.

The Game does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details. Anything the Game needs to remember, such as your settings or the level you reached, stays on your own device. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Our servers do not receive your device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, or gameplay activity from the Game.

Ads and tracking

The Game contains no advertising. It does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or any tool that follows you across apps or websites. Because of that, there is nothing to opt out of and no advertising ID to reset for our games.

We do not sell or share information about players, because we do not have any.

Google Play

You download and update the Game through Google Play. Google collects its own information when you use Google Play, such as your account, purchases, and install and crash statistics that developers can see in aggregate form in Play Console. That collection is controlled by Google, not by us. See the Google Privacy Policy for details and for the choices Google gives you.

Android may also show you permission prompts or privacy settings for your device. The Game does not ask for permissions it does not need.

Children

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not collect it from children either. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about how the Game treats your child's privacy, contact us using the details below.

If you email us

If you write to us with feedback or a question, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply, we do not share it, and we keep it for as long as we need to answer you and handle any follow-up, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over any personal information we hold about you, including the right to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Since we do not hold player data, these rights apply only to emails you have sent us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we release a game with new features. We will post the new version here and change the effective date at the top. If a change means the Game starts collecting data or showing ads, we will update this policy and the game's Google Play listing before that version is released, and we will tell players in the Game.
